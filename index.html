Welcome

Thank you for visiting my academic website. I’m Debarshi Roy, currently an MTech (Research) Scholar at the Microbiome Informatics Lab, Department of Computational Biology, IIIT Delhi. My work lies at the intersection of computational biology, machine learning, and high-performance computing. I am deeply interested in developing scalable computational and algorithmic approaches, supported by high-performance computing and architecture-aware optimization, to study the human microbiome and its role in aging, disease, and precision medicine.

About Me

I have a strong background in Computer Science and Data Science, with research experience across IIIT Delhi, IIT Guwahati, and IISER Bhopal. My academic journey started with a B.Tech in Computer Science and Engineering, and evolved toward computational biology through interdisciplinary research.

I work on microbiome data analysis, machine learning for biological systems, and AI-based predictive modeling for healthcare. My long-term goal is to pursue a Ph.D. in Computational Biology, concentrating on algorithm development, high-performance computing techniques, and architecture-aware systems to enable efficient analysis of large-scale biological and microbiome datasets.

Outside research, I mentor GATE aspirants, enjoy cricket, chess, swimming, drama, and reading non-fiction literature.

Projects

METAQR: Image-Based Analysis in Microbiome (IIIT Delhi)

  • Engineered a scalable clinical diagnostic tool that ultra-compresses 15 GB metagenomic profiles into lightweight, byte-sized patient signatures.
  • Developed and deployed a serverless, cross-platform application for web, iOS, and Android using dynamic QR codes for point-of-care gut ecology visualization.
  • Processed over 70,000 patient samples to build a highly optimized, low-memory clinical database, resulting in a patent filing for the system architecture.

Tools: R, Python, Flet, HTML/JavaScript, Git

NEMESIS: Telemetry-Driven Distributed LLM Scheduler (IIIT Delhi)

  • Engineered a custom multi-objective Kubernetes scheduler that dynamically routes LLM workloads, reducing execution time by 62% in controlled CPU-contention workloads.
  • Implemented topology-aware gang scheduling using inter-rack latency measurements to preserve efficient PyTorch DDP communication across distributed GPU workloads.
  • Integrated real-time NVIDIA NVML telemetry to avoid GPU VRAM fragmentation, cutting compute time by over 40% and isolating network bottlenecks with deterministic latency sweeps.

Tools: Python, Kubernetes, PyTorch DDP, Docker, Linux tc, Bash

ELECTRA-Based Microbiome Representation Learning (IIIT Delhi)

  • Designed a predictive framework using the ELECTRA language-model architecture to extract unstructured patterns from human microbiome metadata.
  • Adapted natural-language pre-training objectives to model complex biological sequences and generate robust embeddings for disease-specific prediction tasks.
  • Built scalable data pipelines to tokenize high-dimensional microbial profiles, improving computational efficiency and baseline diagnostic accuracy.

Tools: Python, PyTorch, Transformers, Pandas, SciPy
